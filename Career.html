    Privacy Policy

    Systech Lighting and Controls Inc. · Last updated: October 2026

    1. Introduction

    Systech Lighting and Controls Inc. (“Systech”, “we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, how we use and protect it, and the choices you have. We handle personal data in accordance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its implementing rules.

    2. Information We Collect

    • Information you give us: your name, company, email address, phone number and the message or project details you submit through our inquiry or contact forms, or send by email or phone.
    • Job applications: details and documents you send us when applying through our Careers page.
    • Technical information: basic browser and device data (such as browser type, language and pages visited) and your display preferences, such as light/dark mode and language, stored on your device.

    3. How We Use Your Information

    • To respond to your inquiries and prepare quotations for lighting, dimming, audio-visual, sound and solar power projects.
    • To deliver, install and support our products and services, including after-sales and 24/7 support.
    • To evaluate job applications.
    • To improve our website, services and communication with customers.
    • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

    4. Cookies and Local Storage

    This website uses a small amount of browser storage to remember preferences such as dark mode and your selected language. You can clear this at any time through your browser settings. Blocking it will not stop you from using the site.

    5. Sharing of Information

    We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

    • Service providers that help us run the website and handle your messages (for example, our email delivery service for form submissions).
    • Our project partners, suppliers and installers, where needed to fulfil your request, under confidentiality obligations.
    • Government agencies or other parties when required by law or to protect our legal rights.

    6. Third-Party Services and Links

    Our website may load fonts from Google Fonts, offer page translation through Google Translate, and link to external sites such as our Facebook page. These services have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices. When you choose a language other than English, the page text is sent to Google for translation.

    7. Data Retention

    We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required by law, then securely delete or anonymize it.

    8. Data Security

    We use reasonable organizational, physical and technical safeguards to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse or disclosure. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

    9. Your Rights

    Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to:

    • Be informed about how your data is processed;
    • Access and obtain a copy of your personal data;
    • Correct inaccurate or outdated data;
    • Object to, or request suspension, removal or blocking of, your data in certain cases;
    • Withdraw consent previously given; and
    • File a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

    10. Children’s Privacy

    Our website and services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

    11. Changes to This Policy

    We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the date it was last updated.
