About our Audiovisual work

Audiovisual, done right by Systech Lighting & Controls

Systech is a lighting, dimming, audiovisual and sound system company focused on project planning, installation, technical support and system design development. Our audiovisual team brings displays, projection, sound and control together into one system that is simple for your people to use.

From boardrooms and classrooms to hotels and auditoriums, we work hand-in-hand with designers and contractors so nothing is left to guesswork on site.
